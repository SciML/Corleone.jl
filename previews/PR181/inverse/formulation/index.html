Problem setup and data

Statistical model

The measured data is modelled as independent Gaussian observations

\[y_{ik} = g_k\!\big(x(t_i;\theta), \theta, t_i\big) + \varepsilon_{ik}, \qquad \varepsilon_{ik}\sim\mathcal N\!\big(0,\sigma_{ik}^2\big),\]

where $x$ solves the model $\dot x=f(x,\theta,t)$, $g$ is the observation map stored in the ParameterEstimationLayer, and $\theta$ collects the tunable initial conditions and model parameters. The negative log-likelihood is therefore a weighted least-squares residual, and problem returns the corresponding NonlinearLeastSquaresProblem. With known $\sigma$ the objective is

\[\min_\theta\ \sum_{i,k}\left(\frac{y_{ik}-g_k(x(t_i;\theta))}{\sigma_{ik}}\right)^2 .\]

Experiments and data containers

ExperimentData owns one or more Experiment objects. Each experiment stores observation times and values either in shared-grid matrix form $y \in \mathbb R^{n_\text{obs}\times n_t}$ or in per-quantity vector-of-vectors form when the measured quantities use different time grids (per_quantity, observation_times_per_quantity). The measurement noise $\sigma$ may be a scalar, a per-quantity/per-observation vector, or a matrix. n_observations and observation_times provide the sizes used to assemble the residual.

Different experiments share the same model and the same tunable parameters, but each may carry its own initial conditions and its own fixed controls (initial_conditions, controls, has_controls). Every experiment is simulated with its own shooting-layer parameter tree so that local shooting states and control schedules do not interfere.

Shooting layers

ParameterEstimationLayer wraps a Corleone shooting layer built from the model ODEProblem. The simplest constructor is

pe = ParameterEstimationLayer(prob, Tsit5(), data)

which builds a ShootingLayer with saveat set so that every observation time is hit. A pre-built layer may also be supplied. Multiple shooting is available by constructing the layer with shooting points; the flat optimization vector then additionally contains the local shooting initial conditions, and the continuity conditions appear as residuals from residual / residuals!. n_shooting_constraints gives their number.

Fixed parameters are represented by coincident lower and upper bounds. Such parameters are not part of the optimization vector and stay at their supplied values. Fixed controls are prescribed parameter histories and are likewise excluded from the optimization variables.

Observation times and model evaluation

model_values evaluates the observation map on a trajectory. A least-squares solver sees a non-finite residual whenever a shooting sub-interval that diverged mid-solve does not reach an observation time; only a trajectory that contains none of the requested times raises an error.
