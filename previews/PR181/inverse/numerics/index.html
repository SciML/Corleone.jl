Fitting, transforms, and regularization

Solver interface

problem returns a ParameterEstimationProblem, an SciMLBase.AbstractNonlinearProblem whose residual maps the flat optimization vector to the weighted observation residuals (plus any shooting-continuity and regularization residuals). It can be passed directly to a NonlinearSolve least-squares solver:

sol = solve(problem(pe), GaussNewton(); abstol = 1e-6, reltol = 1e-6)
est = estimate_parameters(pe, sol)

estimate_parameters unpacks the solution into a ParameterEstimate holding the point estimate, the covariance, standard errors, and confidence intervals. The accessors covariance, standard_errors, confidence_interval, r_squared, adjusted_r_squared, correlation, and condition_number expose the same diagnostics. The covariance is formed from the residual Jacobian at the optimum; inversion of the normal matrix uses a Cholesky factorization with a pseudo-inverse fallback, so rank-deficient parameter combinations are reported instead of crashing.

Parameter transforms

By default the optimizer works on the natural parameters. The transforms keyword instead fits an unconstrained internal variable $\phi$ for every entry of the shared $[u_0;p]$ block, with the natural value recovered as $\theta=\mathrm{from\_internal}(t,\phi)$. Identity is the default, while Affine, Log, and Logit enforce ranges on the natural parameters. Local multiple-shooting states are never transformed. The flat vector accepted and returned by the problem is always expressed in the internal space.

Regularization

The regularization keyword appends a data-independent penalty on the fitted model parameters to the objective. L2Regularizer, L1Regularizer, and ElasticNetRegularizer cover ridge, lasso, and elastic-net penalties. Because the regularization residuals are appended after the observation residuals, the reported uncertainties still refer to the data part of the objective.

Profile likelihood

profile_likelihood computes a likelihood-ratio profile for selected parameters by re-minimizing the residual sum of squares on a grid around the optimum. The returned ProfileLikelihoodEstimate exposes one ProfileCurve per parameter (parameter values, residual sums of squares, minimum, threshold, and the profile confidence interval) together with the asymptotic intervals for cross-checking.
